# CourseNexus — EdTech Course Portal & Student Lead Engine

> **Production-ready, ultra-high-converting EdTech Course Portal & Admissions Engine tailored for Indian Coaching Institutes, Educators, and Skill Accelerators.**

---

## 🌟 Overview & Highlights

**CourseNexus** (`course-nexus`) is an enterprise-grade EdTech portal designed to convert casual website visitors into high-intent student leads and instant paid enrollments. Engineered with vanilla JavaScript, modern Tailwind CSS, and Lucide icons, it delivers blazing fast performance, zero build-step overhead, and an intuitive user experience matching top Indian EdTech giants like Scaler, PhysicsWallah, Unacademy, and Drishti IAS.

---

## 🚀 Core Features Built & Verified

### 1. 🎓 Comprehensive Course Catalog with Dynamic Filtering
- **5 High-Demand Domains:**
  - Full-Stack Coding (MERN, Next.js 14, GenAI SaaS, DSA & System Design)
  - Data Science & AI (LLMOps, PyTorch, Power BI & Advanced SQL)
  - Digital Marketing (Performance Marketing, Meta/Google CBO Ads, SEO Topical Clustering)
  - UPSC / Govt Exams (CSE Prelims + Mains Foundation, SSC CGL Speed Math)
  - UI/UX & Product Design (Figma Design Systems, UX Research, Webflow No-Code)
- **Rich Meta Badges:** Bestseller ribbons, verified faculty credentials, real student ratings, next batch countdown, and tools covered.
- **Real-Time Search Bar:** Instant client-side fuzzy search across course titles, mentors, overviews, and tools.

### 2. 🎬 Interactive Video Lesson Player & Curriculum Drawer
- **Dual-Pane Learning Experience:** Video player on the left, interactive syllabus accordion on the right.
- **Locked vs. Unlocked Module Logic:** Free preview lessons play seamlessly with instant takeaways, while premium lessons trigger conversion prompts.
- **Downloadable Syllabus PDF:** Generates course schedule & module breakdown text/pdf on demand.
- **Direct Mentor Doubt Hotline:** 1-click WhatsApp intent with pre-filled course and lesson names.

### 3. 🎯 Student Lead Capture & 1-on-1 Counseling Engine
- **High-Intent Form Fields:** Captures Full Name, WhatsApp (+91 format), Email, Target Career/Exam, Graduation Year (2025/2026/Working Pro), and Preferred Learning Mode.
- **Instant Inquiry Ticket Generation:** Issues booking tickets (e.g. `LEAD-58392`) and provides direct WhatsApp counseling callback confirmation.
- **Persistent LocalStorage CRM:** Leads are saved to browser storage and inspectable via the built-in **Institute CRM Hub**.

### 4. ⚡ Instant UPI & No-Cost EMI Enrollment Modal (Razorpay / Cashfree Style)
- **Dynamic Scholarship Countdown Timer:** 14:59 ticking timer creating urgency.
- **Multi-Tab Payment Gateway:**
  - **Instant UPI:** Dynamic QR Code generator, 1-click GPay, PhonePe, Paytm apps, and copyable UPI ID (`coursenexus@icici`).
  - **No-Cost 0% EMI:** 3-month, 6-month, and 12-month installment breakdown with HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, and Bajaj Finserv.
  - **Cards & NetBanking:** Clean inputs with real-time feedback.
- **Coupon Engine:** Applies merit discount codes (`SCHOLAR100`, `NEXUS50`) with instant fee reduction.
- **Celebration Confetti & Enrollment Receipt:** Issues Roll No., LMS portal link, and WhatsApp Private Batch invite link.

### 5. 🏆 Interactive Mock Test & Scholarship Quiz Simulator
- **3-Question Diagnostic Test:** Evaluates algorithmic logic, web/AI architecture, and quantitative aptitude.
- **Real-Time Score Calculation:** Instant percentage and merit assessment.
- **Dynamic Scholarship Voucher:** Unlocks ₹3,500 off voucher codes with a 1-click checkout application.

### 6. 📊 Real-Time Social Proof & Lead CRM Drawer
- **Live Activity Toaster:** Periodic real-time enrollment and counseling notifications.
- **Admin Leads Inspector:** Accessible directly from the top header to view total leads, contact numbers, and click-to-WhatsApp actions.
